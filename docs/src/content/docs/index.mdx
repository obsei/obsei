## Why obsei

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Private by design" icon="approve-check">
    Redaction at ingest, pseudonymous authors, an encrypted database, model and sink traffic air-gapped by default.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bring your own" icon="puzzle">
    Your sources, your keys and your model: a classifier that runs inside obsei by default, a small
    decision model, or any OpenAI-compatible model from Ollama to Azure OpenAI.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AI-native" icon="rocket">
    An MCP server: results carry redacted quotes and record ids, never identities, and what agents propose waits for your approval.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Many languages" icon="translate">
    Classified and quoted in its own language. With `obsei[embeddings]`, the same issue is grouped
    into one theme across languages.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
