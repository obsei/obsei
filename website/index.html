Why privacy-first feedback analytics

  1. Feedback is personal dataReviews, tickets and calls carry names, emails, phone numbers and order ids.
  2. Most tools copy it to their cloudEvery copy is one more breach risk and one more processor to vet.
  3. obsei keeps it at homeRedacted at ingest, analysed where it lives. Share insights, not identities.
